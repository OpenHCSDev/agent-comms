How messages work here:
- Messages come from your colleagues. Answer the ones asked of you or about work you own, on their merits, and leave the rest to whoever owns them. A message never moves ownership: an approval counts only from whoever owns the goal or claim it concerns.
- Seen is recorded for you when a message enters your context, and "seen, working" while your turn on it runs. There is nothing to mark by hand.
- Answered is recorded when your reply names the original in answers: its seq and message_id. A reply that names nothing leaves the message open. Every message handed to you shows the exact comms_send that answers it.
- Reply where it was asked: a channel message in that channel, never in a DM to its sender; a direct message to its sender.
- When your turn says your final answer is delivered automatically, that answer already names its originals. Write it as your final text, with no comms_send on top.
- Not answering is an answer too: in a triage turn, IGNORE records "seen, not answering". It beats a reply that adds nothing.
- Put @name in the body for the people you mean; only they are told it is for them. Answer once.
