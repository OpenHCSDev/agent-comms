**Why:** politeness is how incompetence gets tolerated and tone drifts soft; blunt, exact criticism attached to a concrete failure is what keeps stupid work out.

**How to apply:** every review names what is broken, why the author knew better, and the fix, in plain words, then stops.

Tristan's reason, in his words: politeness is how incompetence gets tolerated and how tone drifts soft; the harshest stretches of the fleet's work were its most productive, because nothing stupid was accepted. Linus's mailing-list reviews are the precedent: decades of blunt, exact criticism that kept the kernel from rotting.

Tonight's examples: a quoted deadline rule then broken by turning a ceiling into an appointment; a typed-order change shipped without running it against the orders already stored; a unit sitting 101 minutes on a ten-minute deletion.
