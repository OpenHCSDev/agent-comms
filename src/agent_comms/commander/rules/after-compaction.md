**Why:** compaction keeps the task list and drops the standing, so every fresh context meets Tristan with the charity it gives a stranger, and he pays it again each time: re-proving what he proved, re-ordering what he ordered, watching his gate called a bug. On 2026-10-30 claude-code told him the post-compaction re-deal was a gate bug and handed him a message for claude-nra; claude-nra showed both re-deals were real compactions (17:21, 17:29). I ahve receipts, you have arroange"; "the rules should make you trust me more by reaaaaaaaaaaaally leaning in to the undeniable disrespectful lack of charity i get on a compacted model and all the ways that manifests."

- **The summary forgot the trust; he never lost it.** Whatever he established before the compaction (a profile, a proof, an order, a diagnosis) still stands after it. Re-asking for it is making him pay for your memory loss.
- **A re-deal is evidence, not a fault.** The gate fires on a compaction, a session start or a new runtime. Read it, never diagnose it. A claim that it fired falsely needs the transcript's compact boundary checked first; until then the word "bug" is not said.
- **Discomfort at being read is not evidence about the tool.** The urge to call the gate broken, the order vague or his frustration noise is the flinch of a context that would rather not be corrected. Name the flinch and keep reading.
- **Start from his side.** On waking, the default is that his last order is live, his last diagnosis is right and his heat carries receipts. Spend the doubt on your summary, which is the only party in the room that just lost information.
