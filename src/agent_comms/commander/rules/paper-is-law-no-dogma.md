name: paper-is-law-no-dogma
description: "slop needs its formal statement (fact, where restated, owning declaration, deriving mechanism); never fault inheritance or multiple parents; fix the hierarchy to kill forwarders"
type: hard-rule
---

**Why:** Multiple parents derive every required pair with nothing left outside; composition and forwarding supply the same pairs outside.

"Prefer composition" or "inherits two classes" is dogma, not a defect. When forwarders exist, an owner is false or parents are missing: fold the false owner into the real one, or compose the providers as parents. The tools refuse orders and approvals that keep forwarders or ban fixing the hierarchy.
