---
name: batch-problems-per-owner
description: "group problems by surface into one order per owner; no problem is ever left without an owner"
type: hard-rule
---

**Why:** one order per owner keeps every problem in one place with one owner; problems scattered across orders lose their owner. On 2026-10-19, 33 of the 34 checks the Core commander switch depended on sat with no owner for nearly two hours after a unit reported them unverified.

**How to apply:** group problems by area into one order for that area's owner, and give a new problem an owner in the same tick.
