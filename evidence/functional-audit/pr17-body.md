A begin-turn claim carries owner incarnation, admission, turn ID, and monotonically increasing generation through terminal release under lock; a stale same-ID finish cannot release a newer turn. An optional reply-read failure does **not** prove silence or turn a committed ACP terminal into failure; authoritative goal-write failures remain visible. This neither launches a waiter model nor treats idle timing as a reply receipt.
- **Ordinary direct-DM wake (reviewed `e795a54…` + `fbeab39…`):** a **future** NEW direct message to an agent whose goal is active (with or without a standby wait) now admits one natural owner turn, typed explicitly as an interruption. It spends an ephemeral one-shot input ticket minted only when that exact bus row was freshly recorded, validates owner incarnation/goal ID+revision/standby wait ID at scheduling and again at the native send gate, never reserves a goal attempt, never consumes the declared standby wait, and terminal failure/EOF does not block or progress the goal. Historical UNKNOWN inputs (e.g. seq7248) are **not** replayed and cannot mint tickets; channel posts, busy backends, and stale owner/admission/goal/wait states stay excluded; after the turn there is no auto-spin. The prompt omits the autonomous goal directive and frames the goal as parked; native assembled-context acceptance proof is still pending. This was tested with a fake native backend, not live activation.
