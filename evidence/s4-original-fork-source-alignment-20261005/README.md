## What changed

`require_inherited_creation` corroborates the acquired parent's identity and
original position, including coverage of its own creation prefix.
`common_recorded_source` owns matching source identity/revision. No new lineage
class, table, fields, scanner, enrollment or admission capability.

`RecordedNativeProbe.fork_ancestry` borrows acquired child/checkpoint readers,
borrows all recorded children acquired once by `original_readers` and verifies
every original child prefix. It never opens an unrecorded donor or fabricates a
parent from headers. Prefix refusal closes owned readers through that one
resource. Delivery and construction receive the same reader map. Existing prefix and runtime selection methods are unchanged.

`RecordedNativeProbes.source_alignment` replaces its immediate-parent identity
and revision comparisons. Different immediate parents need an actual recorded
common source position AND every preceding frozen authored input's original
source_delivery evidence, compared in order with multiplicity. Missing older
rows/prefix evidence stay unavailable; matching identity with contradictory
recorded revisions refuses. Same original cut retains its existing support.
`RecallScenario.compare_native` and every direct authored caller now pass the
already acquired stimuli; no reread or current-file substitution.

Parent found the related delivery gap before freeze: source_delivery still
accepted only one immediate fork and checkpoint delivery supplied None. That
whole family is now migrated. `NativeForkCreation.inherits_recorded_entries`
checks original input AND answer membership in every recorded inherited prefix.
Both checkpoint and recall paths supply their original journal and held readers.
The immediate-parent delivery check and its single-prefix/None route are deleted.
Missing ancestry stays unavailable; changed/excluding prefixes refuse. Same-file
original delivery and strict earlier-boundary checks remain intact.

Original configured model/effort, SDK system/tool values, admitted-request and
completion controls remain mandatory. Common ancestry grants no stimulus,
request, independent cost/workflow p95, intervention or study proof by itself.
